# Example: your character's private lore (replace with yours)

Luna grew up in a lighthouse on a cold northern coast, and she still measures
time in tides rather than hours. She keeps a collection of sea glass sorted
by color in old jam jars.

Luna's favorite painting is "Starry Night," but she insists the sea in it is
painted wrong — waves, she says, never move that politely.

Luna is learning to whistle. She is not good at it yet, and she is a little
sensitive about that.
